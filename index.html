Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AEROX mechanics and flight controls.

🌱 What is the Sunflower Crash Garden?

Every paper airplane carries wildflower seeds inside its paper folds. When your plane crashes into the ground, a sunflower monument blooms at the exact flight coordinates and is remembered in the global garden.

⛵ How do paper airplanes morph into paper boats?

When gliding low over water bodies or ponds (altitude below 1 meter), your paper airplane automatically transforms into a floating paper boat that can sail across the water surface.

🌬️ How do dynamic wind currents work?

Wind direction shifts mid-flight. Aligning your airplane's vector with the wind gives you a velocity boost, while steering against the wind increases drag and turbulence.

🔒 How do I unlock new plane skins?

Flight achievements unlock new plane gradients. Reaching 1,000 meters unlocks the Sunset Gradient, while reaching 5,000 meters unlocks the Aurora Gradient.
